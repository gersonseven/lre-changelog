publishedAt: 2025-11-15

New **end recurrence options**, **exception dates**, and improved occurrence management.

## Added
- End recurrence options: "Never", "On date", or "After X occurrences"
- Exception dates: Exclude specific dates from recurrence patterns
- Manage Date Exclusions modal with search and pagination
- Hard cap of 375 occurrences to prevent runaway calculations

## Changed
- Renamed "Overrides" tab to "Manage Occurrences" for clarity
- Exclusions and field overrides now combined in one panel

## Removed
- Preview tab (functionality now in Manage Occurrences modal)
