New **role-based permissions** system for controlling access to settings and recurrence features.

## Added
- User role restriction support: Control who can view/manage settings and recurrence
- Permissions tab in settings for configuring role-based access
- Read-only mode for users who can view but not edit
- Administrator lockout protection for settings permissions
