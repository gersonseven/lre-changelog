New permissions system gives you fine-grained control over who can access recurrence settings.

## What's New

- **Role Restrictions** — Choose which user roles can view or manage settings
- **Permissions Tab** — New settings tab for configuring access
- **Read-Only Mode** — Allow users to view settings without editing
- **Lockout Protection** — Prevents accidentally locking out administrators
